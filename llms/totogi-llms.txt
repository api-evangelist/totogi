- [Understand and preempt](https://totogi.com/telco-ontology/understand-and-preempt/): Transform cross-domain data into governed insight and action.

- [The execution gap (whitepaper)](https://totogi.com/resources/whitepapers/the-execution-gap-why-telco-ai-stalls-between-pilot-and-production/): Whitepaper (explainer): why telco AI stalls between pilot and production; argues the gap is architectural (data access, context, integration) and that an executable ontology grounds agents to act safely across systems.
- [Agent orchestration and shared understanding](https://totogi.com/blog/agent-orchestration-shared-understanding/): Agent orchestration has become the commodity layer of telco AI; what agents still need is one shared, executable model of the telco to reason against — reads DTW Ignite 2026 signals (Deutsche Telekom, TM Forum) and maps the answer to the Totogi Ontology.
